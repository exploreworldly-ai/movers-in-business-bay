Moving and Storage Support

A CLEARER WAY TO PREPARE

How to Organize Your Move

A practical moving plan starts with accurate information and clear expectations.

01

Share the addresses

Provide the pickup and destination locations, preferred date, property type, and any access instructions you already know.

02

List your belongings

Estimate the number of rooms, boxes, furniture pieces, appliances, and fragile items. Mention anything unusually heavy or valuable.

03

Confirm the service scope

Discuss packing, unpacking, furniture dismantling, reassembly, and which tasks you will handle yourself.

04

Coordinate building access

Check lift bookings, loading areas, parking, permissions, and moving windows with both building management teams.

05

Check delivery and placement

Before the team leaves, review the delivered items and confirm that furniture is placed in the intended rooms.

How to Choose a Moving Company in Business Bay

Before booking, compare the service scope and practical arrangements rather than relying on a headline price alone. Ask each provider the same questions so you can understand what is included and where extra charges may apply.

01

Verify relevant credentials. Check applicable business licensing and confirm who will provide the service.

02

Request a written quotation. Make sure the quote reflects your inventory, addresses, date, and requested tasks.

03

Clarify packing and handling. Ask which materials, furniture services, and fragile-item protections are included.

04

Discuss building access. Confirm who coordinates lift bookings, loading access, and any required approvals.

Review exclusions and insurance. Ask about the applicable terms, claims process, schedule, and responsibilities before booking.
